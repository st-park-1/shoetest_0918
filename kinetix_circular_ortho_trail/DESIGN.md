## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality
This design system defines a high-performance intersection between **alpine outdoor technical gear** (gorpcore, hyper-functional durability) and **precision biomechanical orthopedic engineering**. The brand identity is rational, clinical, rugged, and circular. It treats the human foot as a mechanical marvel requiring precise architectural support, and footwear as an engineered modular machine designed to be maintained, repaired, and recycled rather than discarded.

### Target Audience
- **Orthopedic Hikers & Trail Runners:** Athletes and enthusiasts aged 30–55 experiencing plantar fasciitis, severe pronation, or hallux valgus who refuse to settle for uninspired medical shoes or painful commercial hiking boots.
- **Circular Gear Engineers & DIY Enthusiasts:** Conscious consumers, makers, and alpine hobbyists passionate about modular disassembly, gear longevity, self-reliance, and low-waste design.
- **Technical Gorpcore Commuters:** Urban-to-trail explorers seeking technical silhouettes, utilitarian instrumentation, and authentic functional authenticity.

### Emotional Response
The interface evokes:
- **Surgical Precision & Trust:** Utilitarian data visualization, calibrated biomechanical cross-sections, and laboratory-grade validation.
- **Tactile Ruggedness:** Structural resilience, mountain-tested materials, and mechanical interlocking security.
- **Empowerment & Self-Sufficiency:** Confidence in self-repair, clear step-by-step schematics, and transparent lifecycle impact.

### Design Movement
**Alpine Bio-Technical Functionalism** — a hybrid of:
- **Technical Brutalism:** Monospaced data tags, structural micro-grids, explicit seam lines, coordinate axes, and millimeter-grade calipers.
- **Clinical Minimalism:** Generous structural breathing room, sharp contrast, muted mineral surfaces, and high-visibility signal accents reserved strictly for functional status, safety warnings, and primary actions.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & System Roles
The default environment is an immersive, high-contrast dark theme simulating technical mountain equipment displays and orthopedic laboratory monitors. 

- **Primary (`#38E07B` Alpine Lichen Green):** Represents kinetic recovery, bio-ergonomic validation, circular lifecycle pathways, and active operational states. Used for primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, key metrics, and biomechanical alignment lines.
- **Secondary (`#2A5A44` Forest Pine):** Deep alpine foliage tone providing mid-tier containment, pill badges, active secondary selections, card headers, and structural backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#FF6B35` Hi-Vis Safety Orange):** Safety lock alerts, mechanical latch points, emergency warning cues, DIY hazard steps, and high-priority action affordances.
- **Neutral (`#181D20` Slate Graphite):** Root canvas background representing slate geology and technical polymer casing.
- **Rock Gray (`#242B30` & `#2D353B`):** Structural module surfaces, card containers, diagnostic panes, and nested interactive layers.
- **Tech Off-White (`#F7F9F8`):** High-readability clinical text, key vector iconography, metric values, and high-contrast callouts.

### Accessibility & Environmental Ratios
All high-visibility tokens maintain strict WCAG AAA conformance against dark foundation panels:
- `#38E07B` on `#181D20` achieves a contrast ratio exceeding 11.2:1.
- `#FF6B35` on `#181D20` yields 5.8:1, reserved for bold text, warning icons, and visual indicators.
- Text uses `#F7F9F8` (14.5:1) for headlines and primary data, and `#94A3B8` (4.9:1) for auxiliary metadata and caliper sub-labels.

## Typography

### Structural Font Pairing
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its engineered geometry, crisp mechanical cuts, and industrial outdoor character. It commands attention across hero titles, module breakdown headers, and product series labels.
- **Body Text (`Hanken Grotesk`):** A razor-sharp, hyper-legible contemporary grotesque inspired by classic Swiss functionalism. It handles dense medical advisories, assembly instruction passages, and consumer pricing analyses with utmost optical neutrality.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used across technical blueprints, fastener torque ratings, medical diagnostic parameters (e.g., arch flexion angles, pronation degrees, SKU serials), and DIY assembly sequencing badges.

### Typographic Principles
- Monospaced numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enforced across all data tables, pricing comparisons, and sizing charts.
- `label-caps` must always be rendered uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to mimic field gear military-spec stencil engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column dynamic fluid grid with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding and `1.25rem` gutters. Max container width is fixed at `1440px` for technical console balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid stack with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Alignment Rules
- Spacing follows a rigorous base-4 grid system (4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 40px, 64px).
- All technical component containers (diagnostic panels, exploded-view modules) use internal padding of `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- Technical card grids maintain a dense, metric-first density: gaps between related repair parameters never exceed `space-sm`, while gaps between macroscopic sections (e.g., Sole Disassembly vs. Insole Biomechanics) span `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Instrumentation Tiers
This design system avoids blurry drop shadows in favor of **precision surface stratification, hairline containment borders, and selective translucent glow**.

- **Canvas Foundation (`#181D20`):** The primary bedrock surface. Completely flat, zero blur.
- **Level 1 Panels (`#242B30`):** Modular product cards, technical specs, and diagnostic quiz drawers. Supported by a 1px solid perimeter boundary using `#364147`.
- **Level 2 Flyouts & Tooltips (`#2D353B`):** Mechanical lock breakdown callouts, zoom magnifying reticles, and sticky checkout summaries. Shadow is razor-sharp: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 Modals & DIY Video HUDs:** Layered over a `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with background `rgba(24, 29, 32, 0.85)` framed by a high-contrast hairline border (`#38E07B` at 30% alpha).

### Mechanical Border Cues
Interactive modules leverage sub-pixel technical outlines rather than diffuse shadows. When an element receives focus or is active in an exploded state, its border transitions immediately from `#364147` to `#38E07B` (Alpine Lichen) or `#FF6B35` (Lock Engaged).

## Shapes

### Engineered Geometry
With `roundedness: 1` (0.25rem / 4px base radius), the UI projects architectural discipline, CNC-machined toughness, and orthopedic precision. Rounded pills and bulbous curves are avoided for core layout structures to maintain an industrial technical gear aesthetic.

- **Base Components (Cards, Panels, Inputs, Tables):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius. Edges are tight and crisp, reminiscent of modular battery packs, field gear cases, and surgical packaging.
- **Interactive Badges & Status Tags:** `2px` border radius with solid 1px borders.
- **Specialized Tactical Pills (Heel Cup Lock Badges, Eco-Tags):** Max `9999px` strictly for biological/anatomical micro-badges to communicate anatomical softness (e.g., "Wide Toe Box", "Plantar Arch Cushion") without softening the overarching industrial framework.
- **Technical Chamfer Accents:** Primary feature cards may utilize subtle top-right 45-degree angled notches (`clip-path`) to denote removable modular components.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Affordances
- **Primary Technical CTA (Alpine Action):** Solid `#38E07B` background, `#181D20` heavy bold sans text (`Space Grotesk`), 4px border radius, accompanied by monospaced action codes (e.g., `[SYS.ORDER-01]`). Hover state: transforms to `#4ADE80` with a subtle 2px translate offset.
- **Hi-Vis Secondary CTA (Lock / DIY Critical):** Solid `#FF6B35` with `#181D20` text for sole detachment steps, hazardous locking sequences, or emergency stock claims.
- **Ghost Utilitarian Button:** Background transparent, 1px solid border (`#364147`), `#F7F9F8` text. Hover state brings `#242B30` background fill and border color shift to `#38E07B`.

### Orthopedic Diagnostic Chips & Badges
- Displayed in `JetBrains Mono` at `10px` or `11px` uppercase.
- Neutral variant: `#242B30` background, `#94A3B8` text, `#364147` border.
- Active Medical Condition variant: `#2A5A44` background, `#38E07B` text, 1px solid `#38E07B` border (e.g., `[CONDITION: PLANTAR_FASCIITIS]`, `[ARCH: HIGH_RIGID]`).

### Modular Spec Cards & Data Tables
- **Exploded View / Part Cards:** Background `#242B30`, border 1px `#364147`. Header bar contains technical serial number on left and mechanical state badge on right.
- **Data Comparison Tables (Starter Pack vs Refill):** Alternating rows between `#181D20` and `#242B30`, separated by hairline divider rules (`#364147`). All pricing, lifespan calculations, and weight metrics are formatted in monospaced tabular numerals.

### Inputs, Toggles & Calibrated Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Dark inset slate background (`#181D20`), 1px border (`#364147`), text `#F7F9F8`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state engages 1px `#38E07B` glow and label color change.
- **Orthopedic Footwear Checkbox / Radios:** Box shaped with a sharp 2px radius. Selected state displays a high-contrast crosshair or solid `#38E07B` square dot rather than a curved checkmark.

### Specialized Brand Modules
- **Biomechanical Arch Gauge:** A segmented SVG calibration bar showing arch height clearance (Flat, Neutral, High) with millimeter markers and color-coded stress zones.
- **DIY Sole Interlock Status Widget:** An interactive lock graphic illustrating the dual mechanical pin engagement (`LOCKED: 150KG TEST OK` in `#38E07B` or `UNLOCKED: READY_FOR_EXTRACTION` in `#FF6B35`).
- **3-Year Lifecycle & Cost Savings Meter:** Dynamic comparison counter showcasing total expenditure over 36 months, calculating rubber diverted from landfills in kilograms alongside monetary savings.
